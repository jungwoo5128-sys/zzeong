## Background

There are already plenty of JLPT vocabulary apps, but most are desktop web UIs shrunk down to a phone. The buttons are too small to tap with one hand on the subway, and it is hard to tell from a single screen what you should do today.

As the name says (hanson means "one hand" in Korean), Hanson JLPT is built for one-handed use first. Tap a card to see the meaning, judge whether you knew it with large buttons instead of left/right swipes, and see on the home screen in one line how many reviews are left for today.

## What's inside

- Built-in vocabulary data for every level from N5 to N1
- A level test recommends where to start, then you pick the level you want
- Spaced repetition with a five-box Leitner system. Words you missed yesterday come back today, and words you have known for a while move further back.
- Free: 80 reviews per day. Paid (one-time purchase): unlimited
- Works offline, with progress saved on the device

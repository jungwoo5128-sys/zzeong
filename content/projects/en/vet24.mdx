## Background

When a pet suddenly gets sick at night or on a weekend, the most urgent question is which hospital is open right now. Portal search results often had outdated opening hours or were vague about whether a clinic offered night care.

vet24 answers that one question: which animal hospitals near me are seeing patients at this very moment?

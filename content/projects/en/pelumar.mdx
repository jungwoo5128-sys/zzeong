## Background

For a designer at a neighborhood salon, a customer who comes once and never returns is closer to luck than the result of marketing. Designers know they should post short-form videos, but it is hard to also figure out what to film and how to edit it while working on a customer.

Pelumar combines pelu (salon) and mar (marketing). We take care of the whole flow: a customer discovering the designer, coming in for the first time, and coming back again.

## What we do

- Decide what to film, then edit the clips designers shoot on their phones into short-form videos ready to post
- Connect new customers near the salon through university clubs, departments, and student communities
- Tidy up the designer's Naver Place profile: introduction, photos, menu, and search keywords
- Check together for two months whether first-time customers come back

## My part

I run Pelumar with co-founder Seongbin Lee, and I handle operations and data. I built the landing page by hand in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript without a framework, and connected the inquiry form with Google Apps Script so submissions land directly in a Google Sheet.
